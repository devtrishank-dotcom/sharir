💛 Our story

Fitness coaching should be honest and free

Why SHARIR exists

Most fitness apps in India do one of two things: lock the actually-useful features behind a monthly subscription, or flood your workout with ads. Meanwhile a real coach costs more per month than a gym membership.

SHARIR (શરીર — "body") was built on a simple idea: a smart coach can plan like a personal trainer, count macros like a dietician, and nag you like a workout partner — and it can do all of that for free, for everyone, forever. No premium tier. No ads. No "invite 5 friends to unlock".

It's built for how we actually live: dal and roti, not just quinoa; home workouts and local gyms, not just boutique studios; phones that don't cost a lakh. Your streak resets at Indian midnight. Your diet plan respects your veg preference. Your data belongs to you — delete it all with one button, any time.

Principles

What we promise

🆓

Free forever

SHARIR will never put core fitness features behind a paywall or show ads. If we ever charge for anything new, everything you use today stays free.

🔒

Your data is yours

Login with just a mobile number. PIN stored hashed, never displayed. Photos analysed in real time and not stored. Full deletion in-app, instantly.

🎯

Honest coaching

No fake "lose 10kg in 10 days" promises. Real TDEE math, sustainable deficits, progressive overload — the boring stuff that actually works.
