SHARIR — Account Deletion

Delete your account and all associated data

We are sorry to see you go. You can request deletion of your SHARIR account and all associated data at any time.

Steps to delete your account:
1. Send an email to support@sharirapp.com
2. Subject: "Delete my account"
3. Include your registered mobile number in the email

We will delete your account and all associated data — profile details, workouts, meals, weight logs and plans — within 7 days of receiving your request. No data is retained afterwards.

Using the app? The fastest way is Settings → Delete My Account — it deletes everything instantly.
