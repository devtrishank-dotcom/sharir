FAQ

Questions before you start?

Is SHARIR really free?

Yes — 100% free with no ads and no subscription. All features: personal plans, meal scanning, tracking and progress, are included for everyone.

How does the meal photo scan work?

Take a photo of your food and SHARIR identifies the dishes, estimates portions and calculates calories, protein, carbs and fat. You can correct anything before saving. Photos are processed in real time and not stored on our servers.

I'm a beginner / I train at home. Will it work for me?

Yes. The plan adapts to your activity level and goal, includes bodyweight options in the exercise library, and every exercise has media so you know exactly how to perform it.

Does it support vegetarian or vegan diets?

Absolutely — veg, non-veg and vegan preferences are respected strictly. Plans are built from Indian foods like paneer, dal, rajma, tofu, roti, rice and more.

What about my data and privacy?

You log in with just your mobile number and a PIN. Your data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), your PIN is stored only as a salted hash, and you can delete your account and ALL your data instantly from Settings → Delete My Account. See our Privacy Policy.

Which phones are supported?

Android first (Google Play) — iOS is coming soon. The app is lightweight and works great even on older phones.
