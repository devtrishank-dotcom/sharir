SHARIR — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SHARIR ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information when you use our fitness application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name and mobile number (used for login with a PIN you choose).
  • Health & fitness information: age, gender, height, weight, fitness goal, activity level, diet preference, workout logs, meal logs, weight logs and body measurements you enter.
  • Photos: meal photos you choose to scan for nutrition analysis. Photos are processed in real time and are not stored on our servers after analysis.
  • Device information: a push notification token (only if you enable notifications).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To generate your personalised workout and diet plans.
  • To analyse meal photos and estimate calories and macronutrients.
  • To show your progress, streaks and statistics.
  • To send you reminders and notifications (if enabled).

3. Third-Party Processing

To generate plans and analyse meal photos, your relevant profile data and photos are sent to trusted third-party processing providers (such as Google or OpenAI) strictly for processing that request. We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in our database. All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your PIN is stored in hashed form and is never shown publicly.

5. Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you request deletion, your account and all associated data are deleted within 7 days.

6. Account & Data Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, email support@sharirapp.com with the subject "Delete my account" and your registered mobile number. We will delete your profile, workouts, meals, weight logs and plans within 7 days.

More details: Account deletion page

7. Children's Privacy

SHARIR is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy questions, contact us at support@sharirapp.com.
