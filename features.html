How your plan is built

1️⃣

Your numbers

SHARIR calculates your BMR and TDEE (Mifflin-St Jeor) from your profile — the scientific baseline of how many calories you burn.

2️⃣

Goal-based targets

Fat loss: a controlled deficit that steps down over 8 weeks then eases. Muscle gain: a lean surplus. Fitness: maintenance with recomposition focus.

3️⃣

Smart generation

The coach engine picks exercises from our verified master list and meals from the Indian food database, and returns a complete 12-week JSON plan — strictly structured, week by week.
